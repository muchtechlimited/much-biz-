MUCH BIZ – Smart Business Management System

MUCH BIZ is a modern business management platform developed by Much Tech Limited to help small businesses, retailers, and growing enterprises manage their daily operations efficiently.

It provides an integrated solution for managing sales, inventory, customers, invoicing, payments, business reports, and shop operations through a simple and user-friendly interface.

Key Features:

* Sales and transaction management
* Inventory and stock management
* Customer management and statements
* Invoice and receipt generation
* Payment tracking
* Business performance reports
* Revenue and profit monitoring
* Shop notifications
* Support ticket management
* Role-based access and permissions
* Business profile and account settings
* Subscription plans and feature access control
* PDF and CSV exports

Technology Stack:

* Frontend: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite
* Backend: Python, FastAPI
* Database: PostgreSQL / SQLite
* API: RESTful architecture
* Authentication: JWT-based authentication
